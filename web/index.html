How it works

It attacks the way AI reads a photo

01

Editors see through an encoder

Stable Diffusion first compresses a photo into a small internal summary, called a latent. Every edit starts from there.

02

We poison the summary

Over hundreds of tiny steps, BogniGuard nudges pixels so the summary drifts toward a blank gray image, while the photo changes only slightly.

03

The edit falls apart

The model now misreads your protected area, so the part it paints no longer blends in and the forgery shows.

Read this first

It raises the cost. It isn't a guarantee.

  • You can see it up close. The pattern shows as a faint, scaly texture, most of all on smooth areas like skin and fabric.
  • Built for one family of models. It targets the Stable Diffusion 1.x encoder. Other AI editors may not be affected at all.
  • The pattern can be removed. Compression, resizing, re-photographing or deliberate cleanup can weaken or erase it.
  • It only covers new uploads. Copies of a photo already online stay unprotected, and future models may ignore today's patterns.
