Privacy and fair use

What happens to your photo

Your photo is sent to a cloud GPU rented from Modal, which runs the protection and the optional attack test. It is held in memory, not written to storage by BogniGuard, and is deleted within about 15 minutes, or sooner if the server shuts down. Download your result before then.

What we keep

BogniGuard has no accounts and no analytics. To enforce usage limits, it keeps a scrambled code made from your IP address and a count of your recent jobs. It does not store the IP address itself. Modal, as the hosting provider, may keep its own technical logs.

Fair use

  • Only upload photos you own or have permission to use.
  • Do not upload illegal content, or use BogniGuard to harm or impersonate anyone.
  • The attack test uses Stable Diffusion, under the CreativeML OpenRAIL-M licence, with a safety filter on.

No guarantee

BogniGuard raises the cost of AI editing, but it cannot promise a photo will be safe. See the limits on the home page. It is provided as is, without warranty.
